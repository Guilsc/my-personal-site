# OLYMPUS

Olympus is a personal AI operating environment that connects knowledge, initiatives, agents, context, decisions, work, artifacts, and automation. It gives a person and their AI capabilities one coherent place to organize and advance meaningful outcomes.

## Architecture

- **Realms** organize major domains of activity. The initial Realms are Work, Studies, Personal, Automations, and Lab.
- **Initiatives** represent meaningful outcomes, projects, or ongoing efforts within a Realm.
- **Hermes-agent** is Olympus’ Chief Orchestrator (display identity: Hermes). It receives intent, determines relevant context and required capabilities, routes and coordinates work, and returns validated results. It is not a do-everything super-agent and does not hold sovereign authority.
- **Zeus** is Olympus’ sovereign/governance Agent; **Hermes-core** is the underlying Hermes Software/base foundation. These are distinct from Hermes-agent.
- **Pantheon** is the registry of reusable agents and capabilities. Agents belong to Olympus and may serve many Initiatives; Initiatives assemble the capabilities they need.
- **Mnemosyne** is the curated Second Brain: durable knowledge, not raw execution history.
- **System** contains governance, policies, taxonomy, templates, and reusable Skills.

Olympus distinguishes Working Context for the current task, Agent Memory for persistent experiential history, and Mnemosyne for curated, durable knowledge. Memory does not automatically become knowledge: proposed knowledge is reviewed before it becomes canonical.

## North Star

> Agents work for Initiatives. Initiatives generate experience. Valuable experience becomes durable knowledge. Durable knowledge makes every future Initiative and Agent more capable.

**Olympus is the system. Zeus governs. Hermes-agent coordinates. The Pantheon provides capabilities. Mnemosyne remembers what matters.**
