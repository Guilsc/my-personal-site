# Governance & Quality

## Curatia agents / flows
Editorial evaluation, approval, publishing review, evidence review, post-learning.

## Adopted upstream skills
- `grounded-citations`: provenance and evidence verification.
- `copy-editing`: artifact quality pass.
- `humanizer` and `stop-slop`: natural-language quality checks, subordinate to workspace voice.
- `analytics-tracking`: measurement should exist to inform decisions, not collect vanity data.
- `weekly-review-planning`: useful bounded-review pattern for recurring Radar/portfolio reviews.
- `careful`: destructive-action guardrail concepts for production/admin operations.

## Lifecycle boundary
Skills never override Curatia governance:
`Idea -> In Progress -> Ready -> Approved -> Scheduled -> Published`

Ready means required artifacts for the selected Channel + Format are present.

Approval remains an explicit human decision. A skill, model, generated draft, finished visual, or Ready state cannot infer approval.
