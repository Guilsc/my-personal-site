# Discovery & Research

## Curatia agents / flows
Scout, Signals enrichment, Watch loop, Editorial Research.

## Adopted upstream skills
- `ai-trends-scout`: use its time-bounded discovery, source scanning, filtering, and story-worthiness mechanics. Exclude its Samin/Claude/YouTube-specific scope.
- `grounded-citations`: use source grounding, claim/evidence traceability, and verification concepts.
- `arxiv`: specialist academic discovery when research requires papers.
- `blogwatcher`: useful for recurring source monitoring.
- `youtube-research`: use the evidence/outlier research pattern only when YouTube/channel research is relevant; exclude creator-specific tooling by default.
- `ideation`: reuse the principle "consume research first, do not redo deep research" when converting evidence into editorial opportunities.

## Curatia adaptation
Discovery remains broader than a creator's preferences. Preferences affect relevance and interpretation, not what the Scout is allowed to observe. Signals are observations; they do not automatically become Trend Radar items or Content Items.

External factual claims must retain source provenance. Research output should distinguish observation, evidence, inference, and editorial interpretation.
