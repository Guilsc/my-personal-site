## Platform

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and professional peers evaluating Guilherme da Silva Costa. Professional opportunities are the primary outcome; projects and publications provide supporting evidence.

## Product Purpose
Present Guilherme's Business Analysis experience, product delivery, systems/QA background, and applied AI work; help visitors understand his work and connect via LinkedIn or GitHub.

## Operating Context
Public bilingual portfolio (English and Portuguese), with project catalog, project detail pages, expertise evidence, and articles. Existing React 19 / TanStack Start / Vite / Tailwind implementation.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve professional facts, portrait, real project/publication content, GitHub discovery, Curatia public publications API with fallbacks, and bilingual behavior. Public portfolio pages are in scope. Internal Curatia tools and backend integrations are outside the redesign scope. Main is production with automatic Hostinger deployment; use a review branch.

## Brand Commitments
Guilherme da Silva Costa. Business Analysis, product, QA, and applied AI grounded in existing evidence. The user explicitly requested a replacement visual identity; the previous dark palette is not binding.

## Evidence on Hand
src/content/projects.ts, src/content/project-content.ts, src/content/expertise.ts, src/content/linkedin-posts.json, src/lib/i18n.tsx; existing portrait and public repository data. Do not fabricate outcomes, testimonials, employers, credentials, or availability claims.

## Product Principles
- Make professional positioning understandable quickly.
- Use real work and writing as evidence.
- Make professional contact easy to find.
- Preserve bilingual access and graceful content fallbacks.
